Stock Pulse

A real-time Bangladesh stock market monitoring application for investors, traders, analysts, researchers, and market enthusiasts.

About the Project

Stock Pulse is designed to provide a fast, clean, and accurate way to monitor activities across the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) and Chittagong Stock Exchange (CSE). It brings live market data, share statistics, company information, market movers, events, and useful filters into one modern mobile application.

Stock Pulse is a tracking and analysis platform. It is not an investment advisory service and does not support stock trading.

Core Features

Market Dashboard

Live DSE and CSE indexes, trades, volume, value, and market status.

Share Monitoring

Top gainers, losers, active shares, circuit shares, halted shares, and spot shares.

Advanced Filtering

Explore shares by sector, index, category, year end, date range, and sorting options.

Company Details

Price data, financial information, shareholding, historical results, and interactive charts.

Events and News

Track IPOs, AGM, EGM, board meetings, record dates, dividends, and market updates.

Personal Experience

Favorites, notifications, English and Bangla languages, and dark or light themes.

Additional Capabilities

  • Global search by trading code or company name
  • Block trade tracking with date selection
  • Index movers with positive and negative influence percentages
  • AI-powered assistant for market and financial term explanations
  • Configurable automatic refresh intervals
  • Offline favorites, pagination, lazy loading, and error recovery
